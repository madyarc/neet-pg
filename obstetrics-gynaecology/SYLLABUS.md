# Obstetrics & Gynaecology — syllabus checklist

Source: NMC, *Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024* (revised guidelines, notice D-11011/500/2024-AcademicCell, 12 Sept 2024), Volume III. Every topic and competency below is from that document, with its NMC code. NEET-PG has no syllabus of its own: NBEMS says questions come from the MBBS curriculum, which is this document.

**38 topics · 142 competencies** (26 tagged *skill*).

- `[ ]` not covered yet · `[~]` partly covered · `[x]` covered — add the module name after the code when ticking.
- *skill* = NMC lists the competency's domain as skill, attitude or communication only (no **K**nowledge). These are hands-on items, unlikely to be asked as written MCQs, though the knowledge behind them may be.
- Wording is NMC's, lightly cleaned (line breaks and spacing from the PDF).

## Topics

### 1. Demographic and Vital Statistics

- [ ] **OG1.1** Define and discuss birthrate, maternal mortality and morbidity and maternal near miss
- [ ] **OG1.2** Define and discuss perinatal mortality and morbidity including perinatal and neonatal mortality and morbidity audit
- [ ] **OG1.3** Define and discuss still birth and abortion
- [ ] **OG1.4** Define and discuss caesarean audit according to modified Robsons classification
- [ ] **OG1.5** Describe and discuss the national programs relevant to Obstetrics & Gynaecology including JSY, JSSK, birth & death registration, anaemia mukt bharat, SUMAN

### 2. Anatomy of the female reproductive tract (Basic anatomy and embryology)

- [ ] **OG2.1** Describe and discuss the development and anatomy of the female reproductive tract, relationship to other pelvic organs, applied anatomy as related to Obstetrics and Gynaecology.

### 3. Physiology of conception

- [x] **OG3.1** Describe the physiology of ovulation,menstruation,fertilization,impla- ntation and gametogenesis. — *cycle by length and day, LH surge, implantation, hCG, corpus luteum; gametogenesis and fertilisation linked to anatomy embryology: [The Menstrual Cycle, Ovulation and Conception](obstetrics/menstrual-cycle-conception.html)*

### 4. Development of the fetus and the placenta

- [x] **OG4.1** Describe and discuss the basic embryology of fetus, factors influencing fetal growth and development, anatomy and physiology of placenta — *placental functions and hormones, fetal growth and FGR, teratogen periods; placental structure and fetal circulation linked to anatomy; placental weight not given: [The Placenta, Fetal Growth and Maternal Changes](obstetrics/placenta-maternal-changes.html)*

### 5. Preconception counselling

- [ ] **OG5.1** Describe, discuss and identify pre-existing medical disorders and discuss their management DM, HT, renal disorders, SLE, obesity, epilepsy & heart disease
- [ ] **OG5.2** Determine maternal high risk factors and verify immunization status

### 6. Diagnosis of pregnancy

- [ ] **OG6.1** Describe, discuss and demonstrate the clinical features of pregnancy, derive and discuss its differential diagnosis,elaborate the principles underlying and interpret pregnancy tests. *(skill)*

### 7. Maternal Changes in pregnancy

- [x] **OG7.1** Describe and discuss the changes in the genital tract, breast, cardiovascular system, respiratory, haematology, renal and gastro intestinal system in pregnancy — *body map by system and week, supine hypotension, postpartum cardiac output, reference values; uterine size, weight gain and iron indices not given: [The Placenta, Fetal Growth and Maternal Changes](obstetrics/placenta-maternal-changes.html)*

### 8. Antenatal Care

- [ ] **OG8.1** Enumerate, describe and discuss the objectives of antenatal care, assessment of period of gestation; screening for high-risk factors and concept of inverted pyramid of care
- [ ] **OG8.2** Elicit document and present an obstetric history including menstrual history, last menstrual period, previous obstetric history, Comorbid conditions, past medical history and surgical history
- [ ] **OG8.3** Describe, demonstrate, document and perform an obstetrical examination including a general and abdominal examination (symphysio- fundal height & abdominal girth) and clinical monitoring of maternal and fetal well-being
- [ ] **OG8.4** Describe and demonstrate clinical monitoring of maternal and fetal well- being including weight gain, DFMC, NST & BPP
- [ ] **OG8.5** Describe and demonstrate pelvic assessment & evaluation of CPD in a model
- [ ] **OG8.6** Assess and counsel a patient in a simulated environment regarding appropriate nutrition in pregnancy
- [ ] **OG8.7** Enumerate the indications for and types of vaccination in pregnancy
- [ ] **OG8.8** Enumerate the indications and describe the investigations includingthe use of ultrasound in the initial assessment and monitoring in pregnancy
- [ ] **OG8.9** Describe and discuss causes of still births and their management
- [ ] **OG8.10** Describe and discuss cases of post caesarean pregnancy and their management

### 9. Complications in early pregnancy

- [ ] **OG9.1** Classify, define and discusses the aetiology and management of abortions including threatened, incomplete, inevitable, missed and Septic abortion
- [ ] **OG9.2** Classify, define and discusses the aetiology and management of Recurrent pregnancy loss
- [ ] **OG9.3** Describe the steps and observe/ assist in the performance of an MTP evacuation (medical abortion, eva& MVA) *(skill)*
- [ ] **OG9.4** Discuss the aetiology, clinical features, differential diagnosis of acute abdomen in early pregnancy (with a focus on ectopic pregnancy) and enumerate the principles of medical and surgical management
- [ ] **OG9.5** Discuss the clinical features, laboratory investigations, ultrasonography, differential diagnosis, principles of management and follow-up of gestational trophoblastic neoplasms
- [ ] **OG9.6** Describe the etiopathology, impact on maternal and fetal health and principles of management of hyperemesis gravidarum

### 13. Labour

- [ ] **OG13.2** Discuss the mechanism of normal labor
- [ ] **OG13.3** Enumerate and discuss the physiology of normal labor, mechanism of labor in occipito-anterior presentation; monitoring of labor including partogram and labour care guide; conduct of labor, pain relief; principles of induction and acceleration of labor; management of third stage of labor.
- [ ] **OG13.4** Define, describe the causes, pathophysiology, diagnosis, investigations and management of preterm labor, PROM and post-dated pregnancy

### 14. Abnormal Labour

### 15. Operative obstetrics
