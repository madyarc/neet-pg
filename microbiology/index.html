Microbiology

Things to move, not lists to learn: run a Gram stain step by step and watch which cells keep the violet, slide along a growth curve, pass a gene between bacteria three ways, and pick the right sterilisation for each item.

Module 01 · General microbiology

01a

History, the Bacterial Cell, Staining and Growth

Leeuwenhoek to Fleming and Koch's postulates, bacteria beside viruses, fungi, parasites and prions, a bacterial-cell picker with Gram-positive, Gram-negative and acid-fast envelopes, Gram and Ziehl–Neelsen steppers, spores, a growth-curve slider, and shape, oxygen and temperature groups.

 01b

Bacterial Genetics and Laboratory Diagnosis

Mutation and a gene-transfer picker (transformation, transduction, conjugation, transposons), the path of a specimen through the laboratory, culture media by type, biochemical tests, ELISA and agglutination, a PCR cycle counter, MALDI-TOF, and how to collect and send each specimen.

 01c

Sterilisation, Disinfection and the Chain of Infection

Sterilisation, disinfection and antisepsis, a resistance ladder against each germicide level, Spaulding's critical, semicritical and noncritical items, autoclave and hot-air-oven settings, chemical agents, biological indicators, a choose-the-method picker, and a chain-of-infection stepper.
