# Anaesthesiology — syllabus checklist

Source: NMC, *Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024* (revised guidelines, notice D-11011/500/2024-AcademicCell, 12 Sept 2024), Volume III. Every topic and competency below is from that document, with its NMC code. NEET-PG has no syllabus of its own: NBEMS says questions come from the MBBS curriculum, which is this document.

- `[ ]` not covered yet · `[~]` partly covered · `[x]` covered — add the module name after the code when ticking.
- *skill* = NMC lists the competency's domain as skill, attitude or communication only (no **K**nowledge). These are hands-on items, unlikely to be asked as written MCQs, though the knowledge behind them may be.

## Topics

### 1. Anaesthesiology as a specialty

- [ ] **AS1.1** Describe the evolution of Anaesthesiology as a modern specialty
- [ ] **AS1.2** Describe the roles of Anaesthesiologist in the medical profession (including as a peri-operative physician, in the intensive care and high dependency units, in the management of acute and chronic pain, including labour analgesia, in the resuscitation of acutely ill)
- [ ] **AS1.3** Enumerate and describe the principle of ethics as it relates to Anaesthesiology
- [ ] **AS1.4** Describe the prospects of Anaesthesiology as a career

### 2. Cardiopulmonary resuscitation

- [ ] **AS2.1** Enumerate the indications, describe the steps and demonstrate in a simulated environment, Basic Life Support in adults, children and neonates
- [ ] **AS2.2** Enumerate the indications, describe the steps and demonstrate in a simulated environment, Advanced Life Support in adults and children *(skill)*

### 3. Preoperative evaluation and medication

- [ ] **AS3.1** Describe the principles of preoperative evaluation
- [ ] **AS3.2** Elicit, present and document an appropriate history including medication history in a patient undergoing Surgery as it pertains to a preoperative anaesthetic evaluation *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS3.3** Demonstrate and document an appropriate clinical examination in a patient undergoing General Surgery *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS3.4** Choose and interpret appropriate testing for patients undergoing Surgery *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS3.5** Determine the readiness for General Surgery in a patient based on the preoperative evaluation *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS3.6** Choose and write a prescription for appropriate premedications for patients undergoing surgery *(skill)*

### 4. General Anaesthesia

- [ ] **AS4.1** Describe and discuss the pharmacology of drugs used in induction and maintenance of general anaesthesia (including intravenous and inhalation induction agents, opiate and non-opiate analgesics, depolarising and non depolarising muscle relaxants, anticholinesterases)
- [ ] **AS4.2** Describe the anatomy of the airway and its implications for general Anaesthesia
- [ ] **AS4.3** Observe and describe the principles and the practical aspects of induction and maintenance of anesthesia *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS4.4** Observe and describe the principles and the steps/ techniques in maintenance of vital organ functions in patients undergoing surgical competencies *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS4.5** Observe and describe the principles and the steps/ techniques in monitoring patients during anaesthesia *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS4.6** Observe and describe the principles and the steps/ techniques involved in day care anesthesia *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS4.7** Observe and describe the principles and the steps/ techniques involved in anaesthesia outside the operating room *(skill)*

### 5. Regional anaesthesia

- [ ] **AS5.1** Enumerate the indications for and describe the principles of regional anaesthesia (including spinal, epidural and combined)
- [ ] **AS5.2** Describe the correlative anatomy of the brachial plexus, subarachnoid and epidural spaces
- [ ] **AS5.3** Observe and describe the principles and steps/ techniques involved in peripheral nerve blocks commonly used drugs and adjuvant agents in regional anesthesia *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS5.5** Observe and describe the principles and steps/ techniques involved in caudal epidural in adults and children *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS5.6** Observe and describe the principles and steps/ techniques involved in common blocks used in surgery (including brachial plexus blocks) *(skill)*

### 6. Post-anaesthesia recovery

- [ ] **AS6.1** Describe the principles of monitoring and resuscitation in the recovery room *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS6.2** Observe and enumerate the contents of the crash cart and describe the equipment used in the recovery room *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS6.3** Describe the common complications encountered by patients in the recovery room, their recognition and principles of management

### 7. Intensive Care Management

- [ ] **AS7.1** Visit, enumerate and describe the functions of an Intensive Care Unit *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS7.2** Enumerate and describe the criteria for admission and discharge of a patient to an ICU *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS7.3** Observe and describe the management of an unconscious patient *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS7.4** Observe and describe the basic setup process of a ventilator *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS7.5** Observe and describe the principles of monitoring in an ICU *(skill)*

### 8. Pain and its management

- [ ] **AS8.1** Describe the anatomical correlates and physiologic principles of Pain
- [ ] **AS8.2** Elicit and determine the level, quality and quantity of pain and its tolerance in patient or surrogate *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS8.3** Describe the pharmacology and use of drugs in the management of pain
- [ ] **AS8.4** Describe the principles of pain management in palliative care
- [ ] **AS8.5** Describe the principles of pain management in the terminally ill

### 9. Fluids

- [ ] **AS9.1** Establish intravenous access in a simulated environment *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS9.2** Establish central venous access in a simulated environment *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS9.3** Describe the principles of fluid therapy in the preoperative period
- [ ] **AS9.4** Enumerate blood products and describe the use of blood products in the preoperative period

### 10. Patient safety

- [ ] **AS10.1** Enumerate the hazards of incorrect patient positioning
- [ ] **AS10.2** Enumerate the hazards encountered in the perioperative period and steps/techniques taken to prevent them
- [ ] **AS10.3** Describe the role of communication in patient safety
- [ ] **AS10.4** Define and describe common medical and medication errors in anaesthesia

### 11. Oxygen Delivery Devices, Oxygen Therapy and Airway Management

- [ ] **AS11.1** Describe oxygen delivery devices - nasal cannulas, simple face masks, Venturi masks non-rebreathing masks, BVM, and HFNC. Understand the indications in accordance with clinical scenarios. Demonstrate correct setup and usage of oxygen delivery devices ensuring patient safety and device efficiency.
- [ ] **AS11.2** Describe the principles of oxygen therapy, importance of FiO2, flow rate adjustment, monitoring and safety precautions during oxygen therapy. *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS11.3** Describe and demonstrate the techniques of opening the airway (head tilt, chin lift, jaw thrust) in a simulated environment. *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS11.4** Observe and demonstrate correct insertion of oropharyngeal and nasopharyngeal airways. *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS11.5** Enumerate the indications of advanced airway management. *(skill)*
- [ ] **AS11.6** Describe the steps and demonstrate in a simulated environment - manual ventilation by BVM, Endotracheal intubation, and LMA insertion. Ventilation Techniques Explain the principle and settings of mechanical ventilation. *(skill)*
