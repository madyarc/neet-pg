Biochemistry

Things to move, not lists to learn: drag Km and Vmax on a Michaelis–Menten curve and watch an inhibitor pivot the Lineweaver–Burk line; step through glycolysis and the TCA cycle with a running ATP count, cross out the missing enzyme of each glycogen storage disease, and classify glucose and HbA1c results against ADA 2026 and WHO cut-offs.

Module 01 · Enzymes

01a

Enzymes: Concepts, Classes, Kinetics and Inhibition

Cofactors, apo- and holoenzymes, isoenzymes, ribozymes and abzymes; the seven IUBMB classes with EC examples; lock-and-key and induced fit; factors affecting rate; Michaelis–Menten and Lineweaver–Burk with sliders; an inhibition picker showing Km and Vmax on both plots with drug examples; allostery, feedback, phosphorylation, zymogens; enzymes as drugs.

 01b

Enzymes in the Clinic: Serum Markers and Isoenzymes

An enzyme–organ picker (ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, amylase, lipase, CK, LDH, troponin, acid phosphatase, PSA, cholinesterase), a heart-attack marker time course from two sources, the 2018 Fourth Universal Definition of MI as a decision tree, LDH and CK isoenzyme patterns, the Revised Atlanta two-of-three rule, succinylcholine apnoea.

Module 02 · Carbohydrate Chemistry and Metabolism

02a

Carbohydrates, Glycolysis and Pyruvate Dehydrogenase

Mono-, di-, oligo- and polysaccharides as structure cards; lactose intolerance and other digestion disorders; a ten-step glycolysis stepper with a running ATP/NADH count; hexokinase vs glucokinase curves; PFK-1 effectors and the PFK-2 switch; aerobic, anaerobic and red-cell fates with the 2,3-BPG shunt; the PDH complex and its five cofactors; thiamine and PDH deficiency.

 02b

TCA Cycle, Gluconeogenesis and Glycogen

A TCA-cycle stepper counting NADH, FADH₂, GTP and CO₂; an ATP-per-glucose calculator (30–38 explained); gluconeogenesis with its four bypass enzymes and precursors; Cori and glucose–alanine cycles; glycogen synthesis and breakdown under insulin, glucagon and adrenaline; a glycogen storage disease picker (types I–VI and IX).

 02c

Minor Pathways, Glucose Homeostasis and Diagnosis

HMP shunt and G6PD deficiency with haemolysis triggers; uronic acid pathway; galactosaemia, essential fructosuria and hereditary fructose intolerance; the polyol pathway; a fed-to-starvation time slider; insulin vs glucagon; a fasting glucose, OGTT and HbA1c classifier using ADA 2026 and WHO cut-offs.
