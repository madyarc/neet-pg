 PY

Physiology

Membrane potentials you can compute: a Nernst and Goldman calculator with high and low potassium, an action potential step by step, the strength–duration curve. Body fluids with gains and losses moving water between compartments; the neuromuscular junction in myasthenia and Lambert–Eaton; muscle from the cross-bridge to tetanus. Blood: anaemia and jaundice, a clickable clotting cascade, a donor–recipient grid. Heart: the ECG, the cardiac cycle on a slider, blood pressure and shock. Gut: a hormone map, the parietal cell, absorption and liver tests. Lungs: lung volumes, the oxygen curve with its shifts, hypoxia, altitude and diving.
