# Dermatology, Venereology & Leprosy — syllabus checklist

Source: NMC, *Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024* (revised guidelines, notice D-11011/500/2024-AcademicCell, 12 Sept 2024), Volume II. Every topic and competency below is from that document, with its NMC code. NEET-PG has no syllabus of its own: NBEMS says questions come from the MBBS curriculum, which is this document.

**15 topics · 48 competencies** (6 tagged *skill*).

- `[ ]` not covered yet · `[~]` partly covered · `[x]` covered — add the module name after the code when ticking.
- *skill* = NMC lists the competency's domain as skill, attitude or communication only (no **K**nowledge). These are hands-on items, unlikely to be asked as written MCQs, though the knowledge behind them may be.
- Wording is NMC's, lightly cleaned (line breaks and spacing from the PDF).

## Topics

### 1. Acne

- [ ] **DR1.1** Recognize a patient with acne vulgaris including the risk factors, etiology and clinical grading
- [ ] **DR1.2** Device a management plan of a patient with acne

### 2. Vitiligo

- [ ] **DR2.1** Identify and differentiate vitiligo from other causes of hypo pigmented lesions and present a treatment plan

### 3. Papulosquamous disorders

- [x] **DR3.1** Identify and distinguish psoriatic lesions from other causes — *pathogenesis stepper with HLA-Cw6, histology, clinical types, nails, PsA patterns: [Psoriasis](papulosquamous/psoriasis.html); differential picker: [Lichen Planus and Other Papulosquamous Disorders](papulosquamous/lichen-planus.html)*
- [~] **DR3.2** Demonstrate the Grattage test *(skill)* — *knowledge only: grattage sequence (candle wax → last membrane → Auspitz); skill not covered: [Psoriasis](papulosquamous/psoriasis.html)*
- [x] **DR3.3** Devise a treatment plan for a patient with psoriasis and counsel the patient regarding various treatment options and chronicity of disease — *PASI/BSA calculator, NICE CG153 (2012; recommendations as published 2025) ladder (classes and mechanisms, no doses), counselling, drugs that worsen psoriasis: [Psoriasis](papulosquamous/psoriasis.html)*

### 4. Lichen Planus

- [x] **DR4.1** Identify and manage a case of lichen planus — *6 Ps, Wickham striae, sites, histology, variants, HCV meta-analyses, Grinspan syndrome, lichenoid drug eruptions, course and treatment: [Lichen Planus and Other Papulosquamous Disorders](papulosquamous/lichen-planus.html)*

### 5. Scabies

- [ ] **DR5.1** Devise a treatment plan for management of scabies including adverse drug reactions

### 6. Pediculosis

- [ ] **DR6.1** Describe the pathogenesis, diagnostic features and management of pediculosis in adults and children

### 7. Fungal Infections

- [ ] **DR7.1** Demonstrate candida/dermatophytes in fungal scrapings on KOH mount *(skill)*
- [ ] **DR7.2** Manage a case of fungal infections

### 8. Common Viral Infections

- [ ] **DR8.1** Describe the aetiology, microbiology, pathogenesis, clinical presentations and management of common viral infections of the skin in adults and children
- [ ] **DR8.2** Identify and distinguish herpes simplex and herpes labialis from other skin lesions
- [ ] **DR8.3** Identify and distinguish herpes zoster and varicella from other skin lesions
- [ ] **DR8.4** Identify and distinguish viral warts from other skin lesions
- [ ] **DR8.5** Identify and distinguish molluscum contagiosum from other skin lesions

### 9. Leprosy

- [ ] **DR9.1** Describe the epidemiology, clinical features and classification ofLeprosy
- [ ] **DR9.2** Demonstrate an appropriate neurologic examination in Leprosy *(skill)*
- [ ] **DR9.3** Enumerate the indications and observe the performance of a slitskin smear in patients with leprosy
- [ ] **DR9.4** Effectively treat a case of Lepra Reaction
- [ ] **DR9.5** Effectively manage a case of Leprosy based on National Guidelines and WHO Guidelines
- [ ] **DR9.6** Effectively manage complications of Leprosy and counsel regarding disability and stigma

### 10. Sexually Transmitted Diseases

- [ ] **DR10.1** Understand the rationale and Effectively use Syndromic case management for patients presenting with sexually transmitted diseases
- [ ] **DR10.2** Describe the clinical features, stages, and appropriate use of diagnostic tests for diagnosis of Syphilis
- [ ] **DR10.3** Describe the clinical features, stages, and appropriate use of diagnostic tests for diagnosis of non-syphilitic genital ulcer namely chancroid and herpes genitalis
- [ ] **DR10.4** Conduct a proper examination in a patient with GUD ensuring privacy, confidentiality in presence of chaperone *(skill)*
- [ ] **DR10.5** Effectively take sexual history and provide patient education on safe sexual behaviours including pretest counselling for HIV *(skill)*
- [ ] **DR10.6** Effectively manage syphilis and genital ulcer disease based on clinical features and serological tests including in pregnancy and neonates and advise as per syndromic case management
- [ ] **DR10.7** Recognize a patient with LGV and Donovanosis based on clinical findings and provide appropriate therapy using syndromic case management
- [ ] **DR10.8** Describe the etiology, diagnostic and clinical features and management of gonococcal and non-gonococcal urethritis
- [ ] **DR10.9** Effectively manage a patient with urethral discharge and counsel regarding prevention as per syndrome case management guidelines
- [ ] **DR10.10** Diagnose and manage a patient presenting with vaginal discharge as per syndrome case management guidelines
- [ ] **DR10.11** Diagnose and treat a patient with genital warts and provide patient education

### 11. HIV

- [ ] **DR11.1** Diagnose and manage common dermatologic manifestations of HIV including opportunistic infections(OI) based on clinical features and appropriate lab tests
- [ ] **DR11.2** Recognize common dermatological manifestations of ART drugs and initiate primary management appropriately

### 12. Eczemas, Erythroderma and cutaneous adverse drug reactions

- [ ] **DR12.1** Identify common types of eczema (both endogenous and exogenous based on clinical features and history
- [ ] **DR12.2** Provide basic management for common eczemas including topical and systemic therapy
- [ ] **DR12.3** Diagnose Erythroderma, including medical complications and provide appropriate primary care to stabilize a patient before referral
- [ ] **DR12.4** Distinguish adverse drug reactions like Fixed drug eruption, Drug Hypersensitivity syndrome and Steven Johnson syndrome/ Toxic epidermal necrolysis from other skin conditions
- [ ] **DR12.5** Provide primary care in patients with Adverse drug reactions

### 13. Vesiculo- bullous Lesions

- [ ] **DR13.1** Diagnose common auto Immune vesiculo- bullous disorders like pemphigus and bullous pemphigoid based on clinical features and appropriate use of Nikolsky’s sign and Bulla spread sign
- [ ] **DR13.2** Provide primary management for a patient with vesiculo-bullous disorder before referral

### 14. Urticaria Angioedema

- [ ] **DR14.1** Classify urticaria and angioedema and describe etio-pathogenesis, clinical features and precipitating factors
- [ ] **DR14.2** Identify and distinguish urticaria and angioedema from other skin lesions and provide basic management
- [ ] **DR14.3** Demonstrate Dermographism *(skill)*

### 15. Pyoderma

- [ ] **DR15.2** Enumerate the indications and adverse reactions of topical and systemic drugs used in the treatment of pyoderma
