Physiology

Things to move, not lists to learn: change the ions and watch the membrane potential, step through an action potential, add saline and watch the cells shrink or swell.

Module 01 · General physiology, nerve and muscle

01a

The Cell, Membrane Transport and Body Fluids

Organelles, cell signalling, homeostasis and feedback, apoptosis, every way across the membrane, the 60–40–20 compartments, a Darrow–Yannet simulator, ECF and ICF ions, pH and Henderson–Hasselbalch.

 01b

Membrane Potentials: Resting, Action, Conduction

A Nernst and GHK calculator with hyper- and hypokalaemia, the action potential with its channels and refractory periods, all-or-none, the strength–duration curve, nerve fibre types, saltatory conduction.

 01c

The Neuron, Nerve Injury and the Neuromuscular Junction

Neuron and neuroglia, neurotrophins, Seddon and Sunderland, Wallerian degeneration and regrowth, neuromuscular transmission, myasthenia gravis vs Lambert–Eaton, blocking drugs.

 01d

Muscle: Contraction, Fibre Types and Myopathies

Sliding filaments and the length–tension curve, excitation–contraction coupling in skeletal and smooth muscle, the cross-bridge cycle, fibre types, isometric and isotonic, summation and tetanus, myopathies.

Module 02 · Haematology and immunity

02a

Blood, Plasma Proteins and the Blood Cells

Composition and plasma proteins with an oncotic-pressure slider, the haematopoiesis tree, an erythropoiesis stepper, the erythropoietin loop (altitude, kidney failure, polycythaemia vera), the red cell, the white-cell differential from three sources, leucopoiesis, platelets.

 02b

Haemoglobin, Bilirubin and Jaundice, Anaemia and Polycythaemia

HbA, HbA2, HbF and HbS chain by chain, a haem-synthesis stepper, the bilirubin pathway with pre-hepatic, hepatic and post-hepatic jaundice and their lab patterns, WHO anaemia cut-offs, a red-cell indices calculator, polycythaemia.

 02c

Haemostasis, Blood Groups and the Blood Tests

A haemostasis stepper, a coagulation cascade where drugs and disorders show what stops and whether PT or aPTT is prolonged, fibrinolysis, bleeding disorders, ABO and Rh with HDFN, a donor–recipient grid, transfusion reactions, ESR, osmotic fragility, haematocrit, reticulocyte and platelet counts, normal values.

 02d

Immunity: Innate and Adaptive, T and B Cells, Antibodies

Innate vs adaptive, an adaptive-response stepper (MHC, CD4 and CD8 T cells, plasma cells), antibody structure and the five classes, primary and secondary responses, active and passive immunity, complement.

Module 03 · Cardiovascular physiology

03a

Cardiac Electrophysiology and the ECG

Ventricular and SA-node action potentials phase by phase, conduction from the SA node with the ECG building, waves and intervals with a QTc calculator, an axis calculator, rhythm strips of arrhythmias and heart block, infarction and potassium changes.

 03b

The Cardiac Cycle, Heart Sounds and Cardiac Output

A Wiggers diagram you can scrub with valves opening where the pressures cross, a pressure–volume loop with preload, afterload and contractility, heart sounds S1–S4, heart-rate control, cardiac output on a Frank–Starling curve.

 03c

Haemodynamics, Blood Pressure and Its Regulation

Poiseuille and Reynolds calculators, pulse and mean arterial pressure, a baroreflex stepper, autoregulation and the renin–angiotensin cascade, regional circulations, a Starling-forces calculator, shock, syncope and heart failure.

Module 04 · Gastro-intestinal physiology

04a

GI Secretions and Hormones

The enteric plexuses, a gut-hormone map (gastrin, CCK, secretin, GIP, motilin, somatostatin, ghrelin), saliva in two stages, a parietal-cell stepper with PPI and H2-blocker sites, a gastric-phase slider, pancreatic and intestinal juice, gastric and pancreatic function tests.

 04b

Digestion, Absorption, GI Movements and the Gut–Brain Axis

An absorption map from SGLT1 to cubilin (sugars, peptides, fats, iron, B12, calcium, bile salts, water), slow waves along the gut, a migrating-motor-complex stepper, peristalsis, gastric emptying, a defecation-reflex stepper, dietary fibre, gut–brain pathways.

 04c

The Liver, Bile and Applied GI Physiology

Liver functions, bile and bile acids, an enterohepatic-circulation stepper, a liver-test pattern picker (hepatocellular, cholestatic, mixed, alcohol-related), and peptic ulcer, GERD, vomiting, diarrhoea, constipation, adynamic ileus and Hirschsprung disease.

Module 05 · Respiratory physiology

05a

Mechanics of Breathing, Lung Volumes and Lung Function Tests

A quiet breath scrubbed second by second with pleural, alveolar and transpulmonary pressures, a spirogram builder, surfactant and Laplace's law, a dead-space calculator, V/Q from apex to base, DLCO, and a flow–volume loop with obstructive and restrictive presets.

 05b

Transport of Oxygen and Carbon Dioxide, and the Control of Breathing

An oxygen dissociation curve shifted by pH, CO₂, temperature, 2,3-BPG, HbF and CO with live P50, O₂-content and alveolar-gas calculators, CO₂ through a red cell with the chloride shift, Bohr and Haldane, and a chemoreceptor stepper.

 05c

Hypoxia, Cyanosis, Abnormal Breathing, High Altitude and Diving

Dyspnoea, a hypoxia classifier, why anaemia hides cyanosis, asphyxia and drowning, Cheyne–Stokes, Biot's and Kussmaul breathing, oxygen therapy and toxicity, an altitude slider with acclimatisation, and Boyle's law on a dive.
