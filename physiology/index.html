Module 01 · General physiology, nerve and muscle

01a

The Cell, Membrane Transport and Body Fluids

Organelles, cell signalling, homeostasis and feedback, apoptosis, every way across the membrane, the 60–40–20 compartments, a Darrow–Yannet simulator, ECF and ICF ions, pH and Henderson–Hasselbalch.

 01b

Membrane Potentials: Resting, Action, Conduction

A Nernst and GHK calculator with hyper- and hypokalaemia, the action potential with its channels and refractory periods, all-or-none, the strength–duration curve, nerve fibre types, saltatory conduction.

 01c

The Neuron, Nerve Injury and the Neuromuscular Junction

Neuron and neuroglia, neurotrophins, Seddon and Sunderland, Wallerian degeneration and regrowth, neuromuscular transmission, myasthenia gravis vs Lambert–Eaton, blocking drugs.

 01d

Muscle: Contraction, Fibre Types and Myopathies

Sliding filaments and the length–tension curve, excitation–contraction coupling in skeletal and smooth muscle, the cross-bridge cycle, fibre types, isometric and isotonic, summation and tetanus, myopathies.
