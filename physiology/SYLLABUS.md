# Physiology — syllabus checklist

Source: NMC, *Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024* (revised guidelines, notice D-11011/500/2024-AcademicCell, 12 Sept 2024), Volume I. Every topic and competency below is from that document, with its NMC code. NEET-PG has no syllabus of its own: NBEMS says questions come from the MBBS curriculum, which is this document.

**12 topics · 136 competencies** (15 tagged *skill*).

- `[ ]` not covered yet · `[~]` partly covered · `[x]` covered — add the module name after the code when ticking.
- *skill* = NMC lists the competency's domain as skill, attitude or communication only (no **K**nowledge). These are hands-on items, unlikely to be asked as written MCQs, though the knowledge behind them may be.
- Wording is NMC's, lightly cleaned (line breaks and spacing from the PDF).
