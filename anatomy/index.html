The heart, blood vessels and nerves are also from Z-Anatomy, where vessels and nerves are curves with a radius: here each is a tube swept along its curve (calibre approximate); Z-Anatomy has no phrenic or inferior gluteal nerve and no spinal cord (its spinal dura is shown); lymph node groups are drawn as small blobs at their usual sites (no lymph vessels); the heart is shown by its four chambers. This overview uses a lighter version of each bone and muscle than the detailed pages. The left-side muscles and most left-side vessels and nerves are mirror images of the right ones (as in Z-Anatomy), so they can sit slightly off the left bones. It shows the muscles of the regional muscle pages; those pages list what the model leaves out. The four small sesamoid bones of the feet are not included yet. The model has costal cartilages 1–7 only (BodyParts3D does not include the 8th–10th).
