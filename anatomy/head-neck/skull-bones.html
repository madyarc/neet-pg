Based on: Gray's Anatomy of the Human Body (20th ed., 1918), chapter on each bone of the skull; cross-checked with Wikipedia's article on each bone and with StatPearls (NCBI Bookshelf). Where modern sources differ from Gray (closure and fusion ages, names), both are given.

Models used: 3D bones from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan (the shown bone at near full detail, the rest of the skull simplified; shared under the same licence). Small holes (foramina) and fine notches are mostly not modelled; their markers show where the textbooks place them. Contact areas are where the bones of the model touch.
