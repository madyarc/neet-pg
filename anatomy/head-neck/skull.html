The Skull

Twenty-two bones: eight form the cranium and fourteen the face. Tap a bone to select it, tap it again to open it in detail.

The ear ossicles are not counted in the 22. All but one of the bones are immovably joined, mostly at sutures; the mandible is the only movable bone. Tap a bone on the model or in the list.

Bones that touch never share a colour, and left and right members of a pair share one. Colours do repeat on bones far apart, so go by the names on the model.

A suture is an immovable fibrous joint between bones of the skull. Tap a suture or a point.
