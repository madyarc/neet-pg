The Skull

Twenty-two bones: eight form the cranium and fourteen the face. Tap a bone to select it, tap it again to open it in detail.

The ear ossicles are not counted in the 22. All but one of the bones are immovably joined, mostly at sutures; the mandible is the only movable bone. Tap a bone on the model or in the list.

Bones that touch never share a colour, and left and right members of a pair share one. Colours do repeat on bones far apart, so go by the names on the model.

A suture is an immovable fibrous joint between bones of the skull. Tap a suture or a point.
Sutures
Where sutures meet

Suture lines are where the bones of the model touch approximate; the points are found from where the named bones meet.

The main openings in the floor of the cranial cavity, seen from above with the cap of the skull cut away. Tap one to see what passes through it.

Paired openings are marked on the right side only. Faint markers lie under an overhanging edge of bone from this angle; turn the skull to see them. Most foramina are not holes in this model; the markers show where the textbooks place them approximate.

The cap of the skull is cut away. The floor of the cranial cavity steps down in three levels, from front to back. Tap a fossa.
