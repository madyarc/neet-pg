Ophthalmology

Things to try, not lists to learn: place a lesion and watch the field go; change an eye and watch the focus move.

01

The Visual Pathway and Field Defects

Tap a site from the optic nerve to the visual cortex and see what each eye loses: bitemporal, homonymous, quadrantic, macular sparing.

 02

Pupil Reflexes

The light reflex pathway, direct and consensual; shine a light, swing it, show a near object: optic nerve lesion, RAPD, oculomotor palsy, Argyll Robertson and Adie pupils.

 03

Refractive Errors

Myopia, hypermetropia, astigmatism and presbyopia in a ray diagram; correcting lenses, the pinhole, accommodation; amblyopia and refractive surgery.

 04

Diseases of the Optic Nerve

Optic neuritis, papilloedema, ischaemic (NAION and arteritic), LHON and toxic optic neuropathy: the disc, each eye's field, tests and the trial behind each treatment; optic atrophy types.
