# Psychiatry — syllabus checklist

Source: NMC, *Competency Based Medical Education Curriculum 2024* (revised guidelines, notice D-11011/500/2024-AcademicCell, 12 Sept 2024), Volume II. Every topic and competency below is from that document, with its NMC code. NEET-PG has no syllabus of its own: NBEMS says questions come from the MBBS curriculum, which is this document.

**13 topics · 17 competencies** (7 tagged *skill*).

- `[ ]` not covered yet · `[~]` partly covered · `[x]` covered — add the module name after the code when ticking.
- *skill* = NMC lists the competency's domain as skill, attitude or communication only (no **K**nowledge). These are hands-on items, unlikely to be asked as written MCQs, though the knowledge behind them may be.
- Wording is NMC's, lightly cleaned (line breaks and spacing from the PDF).

## Topics

### 1. Introduction to Psychiatry

- [ ] **PS1.1** Describe the classification of psychiatric disorders and its basis (neurotic vs psychotic; organic vs non-organic/functional)
- [ ] **PS1.2** Elicit history in patient presenting with psychiatric disorder(s) *(skill)*
- [ ] **PS1.3** Perform mental status examination (MSE) in patients presenting with psychiatric disorder(s) *(skill)*

### 2. Organic Psychiatric Disorders

- [ ] **PS2.1** Describe common Organic Psychiatric Disorders with emphasis on Delirium & Dementia

### 3. Psychoactive Substance Use Disorders and other addiction disorders

- [ ] **PS3.1** Describe and identify clinical presentation of abuse of alcohol, nicotine and other psychoactive substances prevalent in your area and their management.

### 4. Schizophrenia and other Psychotic disorders

- [x] **PS4.1** Diagnose and manage a case of Schizophrenia at primary care level — *ICD-11/DSM-5 criteria, first-rank symptoms, duration spectrum picker, dopamine pathways, antipsychotics, EPS/NMS, clozapine, catatonia, prognosis: [Schizophrenia and Other Psychotic Disorders](psychosis-mood/schizophrenia-psychosis.html)*

### 5. Depressive disorders

- [x] **PS5.1** Diagnose and manage case of depression at primary care level *(skill)* — *depressive episode ICD-11/DSM-5 counter, antidepressants and their safety, monoamine hypothesis: [Depression and Bipolar Disorder](psychosis-mood/mood-disorders.html)*
- [x] **PS5.2** Identify red flag signs of depression and refer to a specialist *(skill)* — *red flags and suicide-risk assessment (WHO mhGAP 2016), MHCA 2017 s.115: [Depression and Bipolar Disorder](psychosis-mood/mood-disorders.html)*

### 6. Bipolar disorders

- [x] **PS6.1** Diagnose and manage an episode of Mania at primary care level *(skill)* — *mania/hypomania, bipolar I/II, cyclothymia, lithium (NICE CG185 levels) and mood stabilisers: [Depression and Bipolar Disorder](psychosis-mood/mood-disorders.html)*

### 7. Anxiety Disorders (including OCD)

- [ ] **PS7.1** Diagnose and manage anxiety disorders at primary care level *(skill)*

### 8. Stress related disorders

- [ ] **PS8.1** Diagnose stress related disorders (Acute Stress Disorder and Adjustment Disorders) and make appropriate referral

### 9. Psychosexual and Gender Identity Disorders

- [ ] **PS9.1** Describe common psychosexual disorders
- [ ] **PS9.2** Demonstrate knowledge of medico-legal, societal, ethical and humanitarian principles on dealing with LGBTQA+ community.

### 10. Psychiatric Disorders in Childhood and Adolescence

- [ ] **PS10.1** Classify and describe disorders commonly seen in childhood and adolescence with emphasis on ADHD and Autism Spectrum Disorders

### 11. Intellectual Disability Disorder

- [ ] **PS11.1** Describe Intellectual Disability Disorder

### 12. Psychiatric Emergencies

- [ ] **PS12.1** Perform suicide risk assessment *(skill)*

### 13. Therapeutics

- [~] **PS13.1** Describe the process of modified ECT and identify misconceptions associated with ECT — *ECT indications, modified ECT, MHCA s.95; procedure and misconceptions not yet covered: [Depression and Bipolar Disorder](psychosis-mood/mood-disorders.html)*
